02 / The quick answer

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a shopping list organized around product links and comparison details. Depending on its creator, it may include categories, prices, size notes and photo references. Its contents are not a guarantee of stock, quality or authenticity.

Use a list as a starting point for research. Open the current listing, confirm the exact variant and check whether the information still applies. A saved price or an older photo may not match what is available today.

03 / From browsing to a better shortlist

How to use a shopping spreadsheet.

Four checks to turn a collection of links into a practical comparison.

01

Define the item

Choose a category, intended use and budget. A daily sneaker and a statement accessory need different comparison criteria.

02

Verify the listing

Confirm the seller, selected color, materials, current price and stock. Treat incomplete descriptions as questions to resolve.

03

Check fit and photos

Compare measurements with an item you own. If inspection photos are available, review the specific item and variant they show.

04

Estimate delivery costs

Check available delivery methods, charges and purchase conditions. Confirm any current service restrictions directly with the provider.

04 / Details worth comparing

A good find stands up to a closer look.

For Kakobuy finds and other product lists, compare like with like. The same category name can cover very different fits, materials and construction.

Product comparison checklist by category
CategoryWhat to checkUseful comparison habit
Shoes Foot-length guidance, width, upper material and outsole construction. Use the listing’s size chart; do not rely only on an EU or US size label.
Hoodies & sweaters Chest width, body length, shoulder measurements and fabric composition. Compare measurements with a sweatshirt that already fits comfortably.
T-shirts & jerseys Garment dimensions, fabric, printing details and care instructions. Check whether “oversized” describes actual measurements or just styling.
Jackets & sets Lining, closures, sleeve length and separate top and bottom sizing. Allow room for layers and confirm what pieces the listed price includes.
Pants & shorts Waist, rise, inseam, leg opening and fabric stretch. Separate body measurements from garment measurements before comparing.
Headwear & accessories Circumference or dimensions, fastening, materials and finish. For jewelry, verify material information and suitability for sensitive skin.
Underwear Waist measurements, fabric composition, pack quantity and hygiene conditions. Read return restrictions before selecting a size or ordering multiple pieces.
Your cost checklist Item price + domestic delivery + service fees + international shipping + applicable taxes or duties. Include currency conversion costs where relevant; avoid counting charges already included.

06 / Questions, answered

Before you follow
the next link.

Quick explanations for readers exploring a Kakobuy spreadsheet and comparing shopping options.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a shopping list organized around product links and comparison details. Depending on its creator, it may include categories, prices, size notes and photo references. Its contents are not a guarantee of stock, quality or authenticity.

Is this an official Kakobuy website or a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This is an independent editorial guide with links to CAFANS categories. It does not provide a downloadable spreadsheet, operate Kakobuy services or represent the brands illustrated on this page.

How should I compare shoes and clothing?

For shoes, compare the seller’s size chart and foot-length guidance. For clothing, compare garment measurements with something you already own. Check materials, selected variants and current listing photos before deciding.

Do spreadsheet prices include international shipping?

Do not assume they do. A listed price may cover only the item. Check domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, currency conversion and applicable taxes or duties separately.

Do brand names or quality-control photos prove authenticity?

No. A brand label, illustration or quality-control photo does not establish authenticity. For authentic branded goods, use authorized retailers or sellers with independently verifiable provenance.

Where do the links on this page go?

General browsing links lead to the CAFANS homepage. Category links lead to the corresponding CAFANS category pages. Check current product information and purchase conditions at the destination.
